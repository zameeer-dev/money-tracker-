Frequently Asked Questions

How does Pocket-Money Planner work?
It divides your allowance into food, travel, academics, savings, and fun using a student-friendly 35-15-25-15-10 split.
How does the affordability checker work?
It checks your remaining balance, selected category budget, and priority. Essential purchases receive more flexibility than optional ones.
What is the Subscription Trap Detector?
It finds recurring expenses such as OTT, gym, cloud storage, and food-delivery memberships, then calculates their monthly and yearly cost.
Can I customize colors?
Yes. Go to Settings and choose a color palette. Your choice is saved in your browser.
Is investment advice personalized?
The AI assistant provides educational guidance only. For real investments, verify products, risks, and regulations before investing.
